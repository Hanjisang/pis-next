## 4. Conflict resolutions mandated by V2

- Case operational stages remain projections; Case lifecycle is not expanded.
- Technical phase records remain optional facts and are not a mandatory MES workflow.
- Block and Slide stay unified across routine, frozen, cytology, technical-order and external sources.
- INITIAL/REVIEW/AUDIT remain one Diagnosis signing/review process.
- Every sign-out creates one immutable Report; no ReportVersion wrapper.
- Workbench items remain projections from permission, scope and facts; no persisted generic WorkItem.
- Frozen/Routine provenance uses an explicit source relation, not Generic CaseRelation.
- External fee/CA/device/platform/AI failures cannot corrupt or lock the core pathology transaction.

## 6. Recommended wave sequence

1. **Wave 1 — Core clinical closure:** registration/receiving corrections, specimen/grossing gaps, production rework, diagnosis queue gaps, Viewer tools, report re-sign/distribution boundaries.
2. **Wave 2 — Archive, loan, critical value, logistics:** physical inventory/inspection, approvals, package/courier tracking, critical-value acknowledgement and communication history.
3. **Wave 3 — QC, statistics, templates, configuration:** quality dimensions, periods/exports/warnings, BI drill-down, tumor templates, TAT and dictionaries.
4. **Wave 4 — Equipment, consumables, procurement, department space, quality documents.**
5. **Wave 5 — Molecular, regional collaboration, report distribution.**
6. **Wave 6 — HIS/LIS/EMR/PACS/device/CA adapters:** adapter + simulator + observability; no production claim without real environment.
7. **Wave 7 — Data Collection Front Service.**
8. **Wave 8 — Digital Slide Archive and scanner-format adapters.**
9. **Wave 9 — AI provider framework, simulator, result schema, UI and Viewer overlay.**
10. **Wave 10 — Security, migration, performance, backup/recovery and system-wide closure.**

Each wave starts with a fresh origin/main sync, uses an independent commit, runs relevant backend/frontend/E2E/browser acceptance, pushes without force, and stops on semantic conflict.
